# How to — enable and disable an extension

## Prerequisites
A running Neo Gateway with access to the Extensions page and permissions to manage extensions.

## Procedure
1. Open Extensions and inspect the manifest's requested permissions.
2. Enable the extension and wait for its healthy status.
3. Use its documented Neo route to verify a response.
4. Disable the extension and verify that its route is unavailable.

## Troubleshooting
Check dependency errors, settings validation and gateway logs without exposing secrets. Cite the matching product source version. Mark unavailable commands as planned.
